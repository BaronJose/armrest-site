Privacy Policy

Effective October 26, 2026

Armrest is a remote control app for TVs on your home network. It has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no servers of its own. We do not collect, store or share any of your data.

What Armrest stores on your iPhone

To control your TVs, Armrest keeps a few things on your device:

  • Your saved TVs: each TV's name, its device identifier, its local IP address, a list of its installed apps and, for Samsung TVs, the network (MAC) address used to turn the TV on. These are stored in the app's local storage.
  • Pairing credentials: the token or certificate each TV issues when you pair. These are stored in the iOS Keychain. If you use iCloud Keychain, Samsung and Fire TV pairing tokens sync to your other Apple devices through Apple's end-to-end encrypted iCloud Keychain, so you don't have to pair again on a new iPhone. Google TV pairing certificates never leave the device.

We never receive this information, and it is never sent to any third party. Removing a TV in the app deletes its saved details and pairing credentials.

Local network access

iOS asks for permission for Armrest to use your local network. Armrest uses it only to find TVs on your Wi-Fi network and to send them remote control commands directly. Commands go straight from your iPhone to your TV and do not pass through the internet.

Your TV

Your TV's manufacturer may collect data under its own privacy policy, separately from Armrest. Armrest does not control or receive that data.

Children

Armrest collects no personal information from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
