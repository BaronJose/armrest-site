Supported TVs

  • Samsung Smart TVs (Tizen, 2016 and later)
  • Google TV and Android TV (tested on Hisense)
  • Amazon Fire TV and Fire TV Stick

Your iPhone and TV must be on the same Wi-Fi network.

Common questions

Armrest can't find my TV

  • Make sure the TV is on and on the same Wi-Fi network as your iPhone.
  • Check that Local Network access is on: iPhone Settings → Privacy & Security → Local Network → Armrest.
  • Add the TV by IP address. You can find it in the TV's network settings.

Pairing

  • Samsung: choose Allow on the prompt that appears on the TV.
  • Google TV / Android TV: type the code shown on the TV into Armrest. Some TVs keep only one paired remote app at a time, so pairing another app or device can unpair Armrest.
  • Fire TV: type the 4-digit PIN shown on the TV into Armrest.

My Samsung TV won't turn on from Armrest

Turn on the TV's "Power on with Mobile" setting (in the TV's general or network settings; the exact location depends on the model). Some models don't support turning on over the network.

Volume and mute are missing for Fire TV

Fire TV doesn't support volume control from phone apps, so Armrest hides those buttons. Use your TV's remote or HDMI-CEC for volume.

Typing text on Fire TV

Open a search box or text field on the TV first, then type in Armrest.
